Quick Answers

Is your loan consultation really free?

Yes. Consultation is free. We help you compare options from multiple banks and NBFCs and guide you through the process.

How many banks and NBFCs can you compare for me?

We work with 50+ banks and NBFCs, so you can compare interest rates, processing fees and repayment terms in one place.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Chittorgarh, Nimbahera, Kapasan, Begun, Bhadesar, Rawatbhata, Gangrar and nearby areas of Rajasthan.

Can I get a loan with a low CIBIL score?

Some lenders consider applicants with challenging credit profiles, often with different terms. Approval is always at the lender's discretion. Start with our free credit score check and our CIBIL improvement guide.

No. The calculator gives an estimate using the standard EMI formula. Your actual EMI depends on the lender's rate, fees and policies.
