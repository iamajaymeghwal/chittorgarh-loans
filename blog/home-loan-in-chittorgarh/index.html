Types of Properties You Can Finance in Chittorgarh

UIT-Approved Plots and Houses

Property under a UIT (Urban Improvement Trust) approved layout is generally the easiest to finance, since most nationalized banks prefer this cleaner ownership trail.

Gram Panchayat Approved Land

A large share of land around Chittorgarh's outskirts falls under Gram Panchayat approval. Local NBFCs and housing finance companies are typically more comfortable financing this category than large nationalized banks.

Construction on Pre-Owned or Inherited Land

If you already own a plot and want to build, construction loans are disbursed in stages tied to build progress, based on an approved construction plan.

Salaried vs Self-Employed: How Documentation Differs

Salaried applicants (factory/cement plant employees, government staff) typically submit salary slips, Form 16, and 6–12 months of bank statements. Self-employed applicants — especially marble and granite traders — typically need 2–3 years of ITR filings, GST returns, and business bank statements, since lenders assess business income differently to account for seasonal fluctuation.

नौकरीपेशा बनाम स्वरोज़गार: दस्तावेज़ों में अंतर

नौकरीपेशा आवेदकों (फैक्ट्री/सीमेंट प्लांट कर्मचारी, सरकारी कर्मचारी) को आमतौर पर सैलरी स्लिप, Form 16 और 6–12 महीने का बैंक स्टेटमेंट देना होता है। स्वरोज़गार आवेदकों — विशेषकर मार्बल व ग्रेनाइट व्यापारियों — को आमतौर पर 2–3 वर्ष के ITR, GST रिटर्न और बिज़नेस बैंक स्टेटमेंट की ज़रूरत होती है, क्योंकि बैंक व्यापारिक आय का मूल्यांकन मौसमी बदलाव को ध्यान में रखते हुए अलग तरीके से करते हैं।
